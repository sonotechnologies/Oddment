# Oddment — Design Brief

**Project type:** Portfolio concept store. Oddment is a fictional brand — not a real business.
**Purpose:** Show UK independent boutique owners what an online store for *their* shop could look like.
**Prepared for:** Design phase (Claude Design)

---

## 1. The brand

**Oddment** — an independent men's and women's boutique in Manchester's Northern Quarter. Mid-range, hand-picked stock, a bit of character. A physical shop that also sells online.

An *oddment* is a leftover piece of fabric. The name suggests curated, slightly unexpected, one-of-a-kind pieces — not a chain store.

- **Tagline:** "Good things, oddly chosen."
- **Fictional address:** 12 Weaver's Row, Northern Quarter, Manchester M4 1AA
- **Opening hours:** Mon–Sat 10am–6pm · Sun 11am–4pm
- **Currency:** GBP (£)
- **Instagram:** @oddment.mcr (fictional)

## 2. Who this is really for

The people judging this design are UK boutique owners — mostly running a physical shop and selling through Instagram on the side. They need to look at Oddment and think *"that could be my shop."* Every design decision should feel like a real independent boutique, not a tech demo.

## 3. Design direction

**Light, warm, editorial.** This deliberately contrasts with the dark, bold style of the designer's other portfolio pieces, to show range.

**Palette**
- Background: warm off-white, like good paper — `#F5F0E8`
- Primary text: charcoal — `#2B2B2B`
- Clay — `#B5654A`
- Sage — `#8A9A7B`
- Deep accent: oxblood — `#6B2737` (buttons, key highlights, sparingly)

**Typography**
- Headings: an elegant editorial serif (e.g. Fraunces, Instrument Serif or Cormorant)
- Body: a clean, friendly sans (e.g. DM Sans or Karla)
- Handwritten accent: one script font (e.g. Caveat), used *only* for the quirky touches below

**Layout**
- Generous whitespace — let product photography do the work
- Large images, calm grids, nothing cramped
- Mobile-first: most boutique owners will see this on their phone

**The "oddment" touches** — small, personal, used sparingly:
- Hand-drawn underlines on key headings
- Handwritten "staff pick" notes on selected products ("Sam's favourite — wears beautifully")
- A small hand-drawn element here and there (a thread, a needle, a scissor mark)

Restraint matters. Three or four quirky moments across the site feel charming; everywhere feels gimmicky.

## 4. Pages to design

1. **Home** — hero with lifestyle photography, "new in" row, shop by category (Women / Men / Accessories), staff picks, a short "about the shop" teaser, visit-us strip with hours
2. **Shop / category listing** — product grid, filters (size, price, category), sort (new in, price)
3. **Product page** — photo gallery, name, price, size selector showing per-size stock, "only 2 left" low-stock label, size guide link, add to bag, staff note where relevant
4. **Size guide** — modal or page, UK women's (6–18) and men's (XS–XXL)
5. **Bag / cart** — items, quantities, subtotal, delivery estimate
6. **Checkout** — delivery or click & collect choice, details, demo payment, order confirmation
7. **Visit the shop** — address, hours, map, photo of the "shop," how click & collect works
8. **About** — the story of Oddment (fictional, warm, short)
9. **Admin dashboard** — secured, internal. Clean and functional; doesn't need the full brand treatment

## 5. Key moments to get right

- **Low stock** — "Only 2 left in size 10" should feel helpful, not pushy
- **Click & collect** — this is what makes a physical boutique owner lean in. Make the choice between delivery and collection clear and attractive at checkout
- **Delivery costs shown before checkout** — no surprises
- **Instagram link** — a "Message us on Instagram" option for questions (UK boutiques use Instagram DMs, not WhatsApp)

## 6. Imagery

Real photography from Pexels (sourced during the build). Aim for:
- Clean product shots on plain or neutral backgrounds for the grid
- Lifestyle and "shop interior" shots for the homepage and Visit page
- Consistent warm, natural lighting so it feels like one shop's stock

Include a small **"Photography via Pexels"** credit in the footer.

## 7. Honesty line (required)

Footer must include: *"Oddment is a fictional concept store designed and built by Sono Technologies."*
This protects against anyone mistaking it for a real shop.
